The man obsessed with death

An Essay

The man obsessed
with death.

He wasn't always like this, thinking about the ways in which people would be dying, watching the news to see how many car accidents had occurred that day. How many gunshots had been fired and where exactly did the fatal bullet land? Was it delivered to the brain? That's the best way.

No he wasn't always like this, but as he found himself getting older it had become his new obsession. Watching the news for all the different reports from the day, even finding himself getting excited and letting a slight smile flip across his face.

Not somebody you'd like to call a friend, you might say.
▪

But what do we not know? Well, a lot because the truth is he cared very much about life and for living. In fact he would dream about letting loose, socializing over a meal and getting wrapped up in beautiful moments without ever having to have a care in the world. He looked forward to growing alongside his grandson and being there for all those moments of his life.

Yes, what do we not know about this man?
▪

This man was just told he was approved for the organ transplant list, and that at any moment his life could change. He would live that life without having to scrutinize everything, obsessing over the impossible game of staying in balance. He would live the life that would allow him to be carefree.

This man was not obsessed with death. In fact it was the opposite, but in order for him to get his dreams someone else would lose theirs.
